Route Relay Driver puts a driver's day in one app: the loads you are assigned, the paperwork they need and a direct line to dispatch.

YOUR LOADS, ALWAYS CURRENT
Every assigned load with pickup and delivery appointments in the facility's local time, full stop addresses with one-tap navigation, and the load's status as you move through each stop.

PICKUP AND DELIVERY, STEP BY STEP
Check in at every stop: confirm arrival, capture the paperwork and mark departure. Multi-stop and split trips show as one route, so the next stop is always clear.

PAPERWORK FROM YOUR PHONE
Photograph bills of lading, proof of delivery and damage with your camera. The pages attach to the load and reach the office right away.

DISPATCH CHAT
Message dispatch directly, with push notifications and separate alert sounds for new load assignments and for messages.

ALSO IN THE APP
- Fuel card transactions
- Timesheets and leave requests
- Your documents, such as your CDL and medical card, in one place
- Face ID or fingerprint sign-in
- Your loads stay visible without coverage

Route Relay Driver is for Orest Express drivers. Your dispatch office sets up your account; there is no self-signup.
